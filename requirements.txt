- Less CPU/file I/O per second per connected client → the event loop stays responsive even mid-processing.
UI: No visible change. The Logs modal reads result.logs (still complete), and live status/progress in the header updates the same way — but it keeps updating smoothly instead of stalling when a job logs thousands of lines.
